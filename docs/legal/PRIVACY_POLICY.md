## Data we process
Depending on enabled features, the platform can process account email, username, display name, profile media, sessions, security events, Telegram/Discord identifiers, community content, plugin submissions, uploaded files, QA/runtime reports and audit records.

## Purpose
We use this data to authenticate users, provide community functionality, secure accounts, moderate content, operate integrations, maintain system reliability and respond to support/security requests.

## Security data
Security logs may contain timestamps, request IDs, limited device metadata and other operational data needed to investigate incidents. Secrets such as passwords, TOTP secrets, bot tokens and API secrets must not be stored in ordinary logs.

## Third-party providers
The platform may use GitHub, Cloudflare Turnstile, Telegram, Discord, email providers, hosting providers and object storage. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

## Retention
Retention periods should be configured per data class and documented. Security/audit information may need longer retention than routine notifications.

## User controls
Where legally applicable, users may request access, export, correction or deletion of personal information. Account deletion may be limited where records are required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

## Cookies and sessions
Authentication uses secure session cookies. Non-essential analytics should be disabled unless explicitly configured and disclosed.
